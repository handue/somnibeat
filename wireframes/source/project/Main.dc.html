Somnibeat
Research prototype · not a diagnostic tool

One heartbeat-interval file.
A look at last night's sleep.

Get total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake bouts and suspected sleep-apnea minutes, plus how far this kind of estimate can differ from a hospital sleep study.

Your file is never sent to a server; everything is computed in this browser. Expected columns: time_s, rr_ms.
