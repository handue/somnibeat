Known limitations

  • Training and test data come mostly from sleep-clinic patients recorded in hospitals.
  • Accuracy on chest-strap or watch data has not been validated.
  • REM and deep-sleep separation may be much less accurate.
  • Suspected apnea is a screening hint, not a diagnosis.
