      Method & evaluation

      How the report is computed, what it was tested on, and where it can fail.

      Pipeline

      1. 1. RR intervalsTime between heartbeats, from your CSV, cleaned of implausible values and artifacts
      2. 2. 30-second epochsMean, variability and change versus neighbouring epochs
      3. 3. ClassifierGradient boosting and logistic regression, no deep nets
      4. 4. ReportSleep time, efficiency, wake bouts, apnea minutes

      Data

      All datasets are open. Most recordings are from sleep-clinic patients, so results may not carry over to healthy sleepers.

      Evaluation design

      • Splits are by recording and by subject, so one person never appears on both sides.
      • Stage models train on CAP and MIT-BIH and are tested on UCDDB; the apnea model trains on Apnea-ECG and is tested on UCDDB.
      • The test set is never used to fit, normalize, select features, pick thresholds or choose models. Each model predicts it once.
      • Operating thresholds are fixed on out-of-fold predictions from the training data only.
      • Errors are estimate minus reference, with record-level bootstrap 95% intervals and Bland–Altman plots.
      • Every model is compared with a trivial rule scored on the same epochs.
      • Seeds and configs are fixed; one command rebuilds every number.

      Results

      External test on UCDDB. Lower error is better; higher kappa is better.

      Who benefits, and who could be harmed

      Who may benefit

      People who already have overnight heart recordings, sleep researchers and clinicians who want a quick first look at sleep from a heartbeat file.

      Who could be harmed

      Someone falsely reassured when apnea is missed, someone needlessly worried by a false alert, or anyone treating this research prototype as a diagnosis. Real heartbeat files should not appear in public videos or screenshots.

        File problem

        We couldn't use this file

        • The file needs a header row with columns named time_s and rr_ms.
        • RR values should be in milliseconds, one row per heartbeat.
        • time_s is the time of each beat in seconds since the recording started, increasing row by row.
        • The recording needs enough hours of beats to make a report.

        Expected format

        time_s,rr_ms
